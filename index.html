How it works

One API call. Full context.

Your chatbot fires a webhook when it escalates. We do the rest — enrich, summarize, and deliver to wherever your agents work.

01
Chatbot escalates Your bot detects it can't help — user requested a human, confidence dropped, or sentiment turned negative. It fires a webhook to our API with the conversation.
02
We generate the brief Our AI reads the full conversation and produces a structured brief: 3-line summary, sentiment score, detected intent, what the bot tried, and a suggested resolution path.
03
Brief lands in your helpdesk A ticket appears in Freshdesk, HubSpot, Slack, or wherever your agents work — with the AI brief as the first thing they see. Under one second, end to end.
